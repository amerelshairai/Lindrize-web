    Changing pages on slow internet

    Pages switch instantly with no transition. On a fast connection you see nothing at all.

    If a page takes more than 0.3 seconds, a thin teal bar appears at the top so visitors know it's working. If the connection drops, a short message offers "Try again".

    The real site also starts loading a page when a visitor points at or touches its link, so most clicks feel instant.
