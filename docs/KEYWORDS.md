# Search terms

Each page aims at one main search in English and one in Arabic (SPEC 11). The page's title and
description carry those words.

## How these were chosen

- From live web searches on 15 November 2026 for how owners and vendors phrase these needs, in
  English and Arabic.
- **No search-volume numbers were available** (the keyword tool could not connect in this
  session), so this list shows wording, not popularity. After launch, Google Search Console will
  show the searches that really bring visitors, and this list should be revised then.

What the searches showed:

- In English, the common phrase is **"WhatsApp chatbot for travel agencies"**, followed by
  "AI agent for travel agencies" and "AI chatbot for tour operators".
- In Arabic, people write the app names in Arabic letters: **واتساب**, **إنستغرام**, and say
  **شات بوت**, **بوت واتساب** or **الرد التلقائي**.
- For Umrah, the usual words are "Umrah booking software", "rooming list" and "visa tracking".

## The rule for these words

Words such as "chatbot", "شات بوت" and Arabic app names appear **only** in page titles, page
descriptions and FAQ questions. Page text stays plain ("assistant", "system") and keeps app names
in English letters, as decided in the interview. The content check enforces this.
